# Learning log

Three lines every day you work. What you did, what confused you, what you want to
ask.

Newest entry at the top. Keep it short — this should take two minutes, not twenty.

The point of this file is not to prove you worked. It is so that in week 8 you can
read week 1 and see how far you came. Everyone underestimates that, and everyone
regrets not writing it down.
